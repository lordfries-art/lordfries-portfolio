PRIVACY POLICY.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

About this website

LORDFRIES is an independent creative portfolio showcasing illustration, design, printmaking, animation, 3D artwork and other creative projects.

This policy explains how personal information is collected, used and handled when you visit this website or contact LORDFRIES.

Information collected

When you contact LORDFRIES through the website's contact form or by email, you may provide your name, email address, message and any other information you choose to share.

You do not need to provide personal information simply to browse the portfolio.

How your information is used

Information you provide is used to respond to enquiries, discuss potential commissions or collaborations, and maintain correspondence relating to your enquiry.

Your personal information will not be sold or used for unsolicited marketing.

Contact forms and external services

This website uses FormSubmit to process contact form submissions and forward them to lordfries.art@gmail.com. Email correspondence is handled through Gmail, a service provided by Google.

These services may process and store information on servers outside Australia, in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

You can read FormSubmit's privacy policy for further information.

Website hosting and embedded content

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Certain projects may include embedded videos, audio or interactive 3D content provided by external services.

External providers may collect technical information, including IP addresses, or use cookies in accordance with their own privacy practices.

The website may also load fonts from Google Fonts.

Storage and security

Reasonable care is taken to protect information received through this website.

Messages are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to manage enquiries, correspondence and any resulting business obligations.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Access, correction and deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided to LORDFRIES.

Requests can be sent to lordfries.art@gmail.com .

Information will be corrected or deleted where reasonably practicable, subject to applicable legal or recordkeeping requirements.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the website's services, functionality or information-handling practices change.

The latest version will be available on this page.
