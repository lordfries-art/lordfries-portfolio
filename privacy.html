Contact enquiries

When you use the contact form, LORDFRIES receives the name, email address and message you provide. This information is used to respond to your enquiry and communicate about possible work. Do not submit sensitive personal information through the form.

Form provider

Form submissions are processed by FormSubmit, a third-party service, and forwarded to lordfries.art@gmail.com. Read the provider's privacy information at FormSubmit. Google's services may also process email data in accordance with their terms.

Other services

The website may request fonts from Google Fonts and may load externally hosted video or interactive 3D libraries. These providers can receive technical information, including an IP address. Embedded media may have its own privacy practices.

Retention and requests

Enquiry messages are retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond and manage communications. To request access, correction or deletion of contact information, email lordfries.art@gmail.com.
